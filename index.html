✅ Quick Decision Guide

AquaClass Stateroom

Budget-Friendly Wellness

Spa-focused, cozy, and elegant. Perfect for two guests who want Blu dining and Thermal Suite access without paying suite prices.

  • ✔ Blu exclusive restaurant
  • ✔ SEA Thermal Suite access
  • ✔ Infinite Veranda
  • ✔ Wellness amenities

Prime AquaClass

Better Location

Same as AquaClass with premium midship location and better views from higher decks. Best of both worlds!

  • ✔ All AquaClass benefits
  • ✔ Superior location
  • ✔ Higher deck views
  • ✔ More accessibility options

Aqua Sky Suite

Wellness Meets Luxury

The "bridge" between spa and suite. Includes both Blu and The Retreat perks. Ideal for wellness with butler service.

  • ✔ Blu + Luminae dining
  • ✔ Full Retreat access
  • ✔ Butler service
  • ✔ In-suite fitness
  • ✔ Premium packages

Celebrity Suite

Spacious Luxury

Larger, more traditional suite feel with The Retreat access. Excellent for couples or small families valuing space.

  • ✔ 501 sq ft
  • ✔ The Retreat privileges
  • ✔ Butler service
  • ✔ Premium packages
  • ✔ Split bathroom

Royal Suite

Ultimate VIP Experience

True luxury suite with private bedroom, whirlpool, included specialty dining, spirits, and laundry. Full VIP cruise experience.

  • ✔ 759 sq ft
  • ✔ Separate bedroom
  • ✔ Unlimited specialty dining
  • ✔ Whirlpool tub
  • ✔ Included laundry
  • ✔ Premium spirits & bar
