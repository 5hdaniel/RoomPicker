✅ Quick Decision Guide

AquaClass Stateroom

Budget-Friendly Wellness

Spa-focused, cozy, and elegant. Perfect for two guests who want Blu dining and Thermal Suite access without paying suite prices.

  • ✔ Blu exclusive restaurant
  • ✔ SEA Thermal Suite access
  • ✔ Infinite Veranda
  • ✔ Wellness amenities

Prime AquaClass

Better Location

Same as AquaClass with premium midship location and better views from higher decks. Best of both worlds!

  • ✔ All AquaClass benefits
  • ✔ Superior location
  • ✔ Higher deck views
  • ✔ More accessibility options

Aqua Sky Suite

Wellness Meets Luxury

The "bridge" between spa and suite. Includes both Blu and The Retreat perks. Ideal for wellness with butler service.

  • ✔ Blu + Luminae dining
  • ✔ Full Retreat access
  • ✔ Butler service
  • ✔ In-suite fitness
  • ✔ Premium packages

Magic Carpet Sky Suite

Unique Views

Studio suite with spectacular views of Celebrity's innovative Magic Carpet platform. Full Retreat access with butler service.

  • ✔ 400 sq ft studio
  • ✔ Unique Magic Carpet views
  • ✔ The Retreat privileges
  • ✔ Butler with Butler Chat
  • ✔ Luminae dining
  • ✔ Premium packages

Sky Suite

High-Deck Paradise

Elevated studio suite on high-deck with panoramic ocean views. Perfect for families or couples wanting Retreat luxury.

  • ✔ 398-517 sq ft

  • ✔ Luminae dining
  • ✔ Up to 4 guests

Sunset Sky Suite

Romantic Sunsets

Large aft-facing studio suite designed for spectacular sunset views. Perfect for romantic getaways with full Retreat service.

Spacious Luxury
