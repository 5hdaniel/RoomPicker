Privacy Statement

Last Updated: November 11, 2024

Our Commitment to Privacy

This privacy policy explains how Celebrity Edge-Class Room Picker ("we", "us", or "our") collects and uses information when you visit our website.

Analytics and Data Collection

We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve and market our products/services. Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to determine the popularity of products/services and online activity. Additionally, we use this information for site optimization, fraud/security purposes, and advertising.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our website. Clarity collects:

  • Session replays showing how you navigate and interact with our quiz
  • Heatmaps indicating where users click and scroll
  • Behavioral metrics such as time spent on pages and user flow patterns
  • Technical information about your device and browser

Microsoft Clarity is a privacy-friendly tool that does not use cookies and is GDPR-compliant.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to track:

  • Quiz interactions (answers selected, completion rates)
  • Room recommendations and user preferences
  • Navigation patterns (back button usage, quiz restarts)
  • General website traffic and visitor demographics

What Data We Collect

When you use our website, we collect:

  • Usage Information: Quiz answers, room recommendations, button clicks, and navigation patterns
  • Technical Information: Browser type, device type, IP address, and referring website
  • Interaction Data: Mouse movements, clicks, scrolling behavior, and time spent on pages

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:

  • Improve the quiz experience and room recommendations
  • Understand which features are most valuable to users
  • Optimize website performance and user experience
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Analyze user behavior patterns to enhance our services

Data Sharing

Your data is shared with:

  • Microsoft: Through Microsoft Clarity for analytics purposes
  • Google: Through Google Analytics for website analytics

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

For information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Privacy Choices

You can control data collection by:

  • Using browser settings to block or delete cookies
  • Using browser extensions that block tracking scripts
  • Opting out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Adjusting your browser's Do Not Track settings

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the data collected through our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

No Personal Information Required

Our website does not require you to provide any personal information such as name, email, or contact details. The quiz is completely anonymous.

Third-Party Links

Our website is not affiliated with Celebrity Cruises. Any booking or reservation must be made directly with Celebrity Cruises, and their privacy policy will apply to any personal information you provide to them.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us through the website.
